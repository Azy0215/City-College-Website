OFFICES

CCSJDM OFFICES

Our offices provide academic, administrative, student, health, library, and technology services to support the CCSJDM community.

📋

REGISTRAR OFFICE

The Registrar Office is responsible for maintaining student records, processing enrollment documents, issuing academic credentials, and providing administrative support.

📝

ADMISSIONS OFFICE

The Admissions Office assists prospective students with application procedures, admission requirements, enrollment concerns, and program information.

📚

LIBRARY

The Library serves as a center for learning, research, and academic excellence. It provides books, digital resources, journals, and study spaces.

🎓

STUDENT AFFAIRS OFFICE

The Student Affairs Office supports student development through leadership programs, campus activities, student organizations, counseling services, and welfare initiatives.

🏥

CLINIC

The Clinic Office promotes the health, safety, and well-being of students, faculty, and staff. It provides medical consultations, first aid, health monitoring, and wellness programs.

💻

MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM (MIS) OFFICE

The Management Information System (MIS) Office manages the college's technology infrastructure, information systems, and digital services. It also provides technical support and helps maintain institutional data and digital systems.
