ABOUT CCSJDM

VISION STATEMENT

City College of San Jose Del Monte is an institution that is driven to provide excellent educational opportunities responsive to the needs of the society and committed to continuously sustain quality education anchored on exemplary leadership, dedicated service, accountability, and with deep sense of professionalism.

OUR MISSION

1

Enhance students technical and academic competence vital in providing immediate response to their needs.

2

Create new programs and services with high level of excellence and standards.

3

Strengthen cultural and societal understanding through language and the arts, culture, and heritage.

4

Develop a robust community and industry linkages and partnership.

5

Engage in co-curricular and extra-curricular endeavors that would harness the human skills of all members.

6

Promote continuous improvement by regularly conducting evaluation on programs, services, systems, and policies.
